What this page can’t tell you

Of 799 blocks, 151 have tickets but no rule we can point to (a sign probably exists that the city’s map doesn’t carry), and 154 have no records at all. No record is not the same as free parking. No dataset covers red curbs, hydrants, driveways, loading zones, or temporary no-parking signs. Terms & data notes explain what is inferred and what is official.
